## Weight Management

- Weight is currently described as fine by the specialist
- Maintaining lean body condition is one of the two most important factors in arthritis management (alongside activity)
- Weight monitoring should continue at routine vet visits

## Joint-Supportive Options

### Omega-3 Fatty Acids
- **Evidence:** Moderate evidence for benefit in canine OA
- **Sources:** Fish oil supplements, or as ingredient in mobility diets
- Salmon oil already included in current supplement regimen

### Glucosamine and Chondroitin
- **Evidence:** Weak — limited data on efficacy
- **Risk:** Very low
- Subjective improvement reported by many owners; reasonable to continue

### Joint Mobility Diets
- Some prescription veterinary diets are formulated with added omega-3s and joint support
- Non-prescription versions available in pet shops
- Some are also formulated for weight loss (not currently needed)
- Vet can advise on specific brands

### Vet-Specific Supplements
- Some joint supplements are only available via a vet — generally higher-quality formulations
- Worth asking the local vet for their recommendation

## Specialist Recommendations (2026-05)

- Omega-3s — worth adding (salmon is included in current Butternut Box rotation; salmon oil supplement also in use)
- Joint supplement — worth trying (low harm, possible benefit; already included)
- Mobility diet — optional; could replace separate supplements; Butternut Box provides fresh whole-food ingredients
- No urgent specific diet change needed
