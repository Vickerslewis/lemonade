**Reason:** investigation of an earlier lameness episode (see the [visit](/visits/2022-10-20-lameness-investigation/))

## Findings

- **Elbows (multiple views, both sides):** bilateral elbow arthrosis — osteophyte formation; likely cause of the lameness at the time (per the vetXS report)
- **Pelvis (VD and lateral):** bilateral coxofemoral (hip) arthrosis — less clinically significant; no dysplastic joint shape

> [!note]
> The carpus and paws were not imaged at this visit — a gap only resolved by the 2026 CT.

> [!warning]
> The 2026 CT found the elbows completely normal (no arthrosis). This 2022 finding hasn't been formally reconciled with the CT — see [open questions](/open-questions/).
