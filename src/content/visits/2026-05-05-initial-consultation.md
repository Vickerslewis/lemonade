**Type:** Pre-imaging consultation

## History reported

- Left forelimb injury approximately 3 years ago — X-rays performed; fully resolved at the time
- ~3 weeks before presentation: tripped outside; seemed fine immediately but became lame on hard ground on the way back from the walk
- Initially treated with meloxicam — improvement on soft ground; remained slow on hard ground
- Local vet X-rays showed a proliferative lesion in the distal 5th metacarpal bone
- Missed 2 days of meloxicam without significant worsening; continued to improve; no significant exercise yet at presentation

## Physical examination

- Symmetrical gait
- Neurological and pelvic limb examinations unremarkable
- No abnormalities of shoulders, elbows or carpi
- Right foot: metacarpophalangeal joint swelling of the 5th and 2nd digits; no discomfort
- Left foot: ~1.5 cm hard swelling proximal to the 5th MCP joint; no reaction on palpation; no instability; no local lymph node enlargement

## Differential diagnoses discussed

1. Metacarpophalangeal osteoarthritis
2. Old fracture
3. Osteomyelitis
4. Bone cyst
5. Neoplastic lesion
