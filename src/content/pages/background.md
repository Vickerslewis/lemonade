## About him

- Male Golden Retriever, born 16 November 2019; with his owner since he was a puppy (~8 weeks)
- Adventurous from a young age
- Loves the outdoors — "would live outside if he could"
- Active hiker; loves swimming
- Strong appetite; enjoys his food

## Health history

### Puppy

- Possible panosteitis ("growing pains") — supported by an old change in the left humerus seen on the 2026 CT
- Otherwise standard puppy growth

### Young adult

- Lots of hiking and outdoor activity — possibly contributed to the knuckle-joint arthritis

### Adult

- Continued active lifestyle; various minor injuries over the years
- 2022: lameness episode investigated (see [imaging](/imaging/))
- 2025–2026: epileptic seizures began (see the [seizure log](/seizures/))

## Personality and behaviour

- Wants to be outside whenever possible
- Generally calm, gentle and easy-going; very people-oriented
- A bit of a nervous dog — some anxieties around small or confined spaces and car travel
- Doesn't like the noise of wind; the flat is windy, so doors are taped and draught stoppers used
- Sensitive to light and noise around seizures — managed at home
- Excitable and adrenaline-driven — can mask discomfort by pushing through pain
- Recovers well from procedures — eating and walking soon after

## Useful context for vets

- Highly motivated outdoors — will run on three legs for grass
- May not self-limit when in pain; adrenaline can override discomfort
- Tolerates handling well
- Tolerates anaesthesia well, with occasional post-procedure urinary leakage (bladder management — not a clinical concern). Seizure 3 followed a general anaesthetic, so a seizure risk after anaesthesia has been noted
- Has never travelled abroad
