> MCT oil (medium-chain triglycerides) gives the brain an alternative energy source. There is some evidence it reduces seizure frequency in dogs. Lemonade has had it daily since his first seizure, and Northwest endorsed it on 26 October 2026. It is **not** the same as coconut oil. The alternative is a diet that contains MCT, but that would mean changing his whole diet. Northwest also confirmed there is no evidence for avoiding pork, beef or gluten for epilepsy.

## Joint-supportive options

- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — moderate evidence for benefit in canine osteoarthritis; from fish oil or mobility diets. Salmon oil is already in use
- **Glucosamine and chondroitin** — weak evidence, very low risk; many owners report improvement, reasonable to continue
- **Joint mobility diets** — some prescription diets add omega-3s and joint support; non-prescription versions exist; some are also for weight loss (not needed)
- **Vet-only supplements** — generally higher-quality formulations; worth asking the local vet

## Specialist recommendations (May 2026)

- Joint supplement — worth trying (low harm, possible benefit; already included)
