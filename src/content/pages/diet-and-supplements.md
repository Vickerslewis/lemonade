## Current diet

- **Food:** Butternut Box — fresh cooked wet food, 800 g/day
- **Proteins:** chicken, lamb and beef, rotated
- **Water intake:** normal

> [!note]
> The specialist confirmed his weight is fine. Keeping him lean is one of the two most important factors in managing arthritis (alongside activity). Even 1–2 kg above ideal noticeably increases joint load.
