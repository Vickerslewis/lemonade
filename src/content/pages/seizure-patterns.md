- **Time of day:** all five seizures happened at night or during sleep
- **Gaps between episodes:** 55, then 105, then 177 days — lengthening until the cluster on 22 October 2026
- **Cluster:** seizures 4 and 5 were ~5 h 47 min apart; emergency vet attended, vitals clear
- **Warning signs:** seizures 1, 2 and 4 were preceded by ~20–24 hours of anxiety, pacing and staring — a consistent prodrome. Some warning-sign episodes have passed without a seizure
- **Afterwards:** aggressive or defensive behaviour after seizures 1, 2 and 4; absent after 3 and 5. Recovery is getting quicker (barking settles in ~15 minutes, down from up to ~40)
- **Possible triggers:** seizure 3 followed a general anaesthetic; wind and household noise may play a part (seizures 1 and 2). No toxins, diet changes or missed medications for any episode
