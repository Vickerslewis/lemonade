## Current plan

Weekly acupuncture at Vets for Pets Salford (since 18 June 2026), the boot on his left foot for pavement walks, and swimming.

## Active

### Acupuncture

Every week at **Vets for Pets Salford** (Inside Pets at Home, 100 Regent Road, Salford). He enjoys it and sleeps through the whole session.

It helps with a range of things:

- **Arthritis** — reduces pain and inflammation in his knuckle joints. No arthritis problems since May 2026
- **Epilepsy** — part of his care alongside the seizure plan
- **Digestion** — there are signs it helps his diet and digestion, which matters for his anal glands

First recommended by Nick Macdonald (Northwest Veterinary Specialists) for his arthritis. It started on 18 June 2026 with 5 booked sessions and has continued weekly since.

### Boot (left foot)

- For pavement and hard-surface walks — left foot only (the affected side)
- Protects the foot and allows more pain-free use on hard ground
- Not needed on grass

### Swimming

- Non-weight-bearing exercise, good for joint mobility; recommended by the specialist

## Future options

### K-laser therapy

- **What:** Class IV laser therapy — promotes soft tissue healing and reduces inflammation
- **When:** if acupuncture doesn't settle inflammation enough
- **Frequency:** as advised by the physiotherapist
- **Comfort:** not painful; may feel slightly warm; most dogs tolerate it well
- **How:** ask Vets for Pets Salford for a physiotherapist referral, or Northwest if they have a laser facility

### Intra-articular injections

- Steroid, hyaluronic acid, or stem cell/PRP — not currently indicated; to consider if conservative treatment fails
