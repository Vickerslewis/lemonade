## Clinical Notes

> [!note]
> Onsior and paracetamol can be safely given together.

> [!note]
> The specialist referral letter (2026-05-13) recommended "paracetamol in addition to the meloxicam." Resolved 2026-10-26: no NSAID is being given, and paracetamol was a short course only. The Onsior and paracetamol notes here are kept for reference if they're needed again.

> [!note]
> Long-term NSAID use (Onsior) is safe with periodic blood monitoring and can be continued indefinitely if needed.

> [!warning]
> Paracetamol is safe for dogs at the prescribed dose. **Never give to cats** — it is lethal.

> [!tip]
> Do not stop the paracetamol course too early. Inflammation outlasts pain symptoms; consider continuing for ~2 weeks after symptoms resolve to prevent immediate relapse.

> [!tip]
> When introducing new medications, add one at a time where possible so the effect of each can be assessed individually.

> [!note]
> Levetiracetam is a human medicine used off-label in dogs. It is generally very well tolerated; occasional sleepiness, and wobbliness after large doses. Behaviour changes are usually positive (Lemonade is noticeably calmer on it).
