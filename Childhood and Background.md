# Childhood and Background

> Early life, temperament, and context for understanding Lemonade's current health.

## About Lemonade

- Male Golden Retriever, born 16 November 2019
- Adventurous from a young age
- Loves the outdoors — "would live outside if he could"
- Active hiker
- Loves swimming
- Strong appetite; enjoys his food

## Relevant Health History

### Juvenile Period
- Possible panosteitis ("growing pains") — supported by an old change in the left humerus seen on 2026 CT
- Standard puppy growth without obvious incident

### Adolescence and Young Adult
- Lots of hiking and outdoor activity
- Possibly contributed to current bilateral knuckle joint arthritis

### Adulthood
- Continued active lifestyle
- Various minor injuries over the years
- 2022: Lameness episode investigated (see [[Imaging History]])
- 2025–2026: Onset of epileptic seizures (see [[Seizure Log]])

## Personality and Behaviour

- Wants to be outside whenever possible
- Recovers well from procedures — eating and walking soon after
- Sensitive to environmental triggers around seizures (lights, noise) — managed at home
- Excitable and adrenaline-driven — can mask discomfort by pushing through pain
- Generally calm, gentle, and easy-going; very people-oriented
- Some anxieties: small or confined spaces, car travel

## Useful Context for Vets

- Highly motivated outdoors — will run on three legs for grass
- May not self-limit when in pain; adrenaline can override discomfort
- Tolerates handling well
- Tolerates anaesthesia well, with occasional post-procedure urinary leakage (bladder management — not a clinical concern)
